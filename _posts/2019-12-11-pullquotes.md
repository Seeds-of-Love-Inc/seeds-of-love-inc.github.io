[NorthWind Family Ministries](https://www.northwindfm.org/) respectfully acknowledges its activities occur on the traditional lands of Indigenous Peoples. NorthWind’s facilities are located on the traditional lands of the Fort William First Nation, who are signatories to the Robinson Superior Treaty of 1850.<br><br>

NorthWind acknowledges the presence of many Indigenous Peoples in the area, including those from Treaty 3, Treaty 5, and Treaty 9. NorthWind is committed to a respectful relationship with First Nations, Métis, and Inuit Peoples.
